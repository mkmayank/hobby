| **Code / Number** | **Example** | **Meaning** |
|------------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Toy# / Product Code** | GRH87 | Mattel catalog ID for the casting (same for all variants). |
| **Mainline Number** | 108/250 | Car’s position in the yearly 250-car lineup. |
| **Series Number** | 4/5 | Car’s spot in a mini-series (e.g., 4th of 5 in HW Rescue). |
| **Base Code** | U17N | Production date & plant: **U=2024**, **17=week**, **N=factory**. |
| **Case Assortment** | Case F | Release wave (A–Q, skipping I); each case = ~72 cars. |


> Many companies skip letters like I or O in codes to avoid confusing them with 1 and 0

Since 2013, Mattel has incorporated Treasure Hunts into other series instead of being their own series.

Treasure Hunts are distinguished by the 'circle flame' symbol.
