
| Name | Meaning | Years |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Stone Age | Humans mainly used stone tools; hunting, gathering, early farming | ~3.3 million BCE – 3300 BCE |
| Paleolithic (Old Stone Age) | Earliest humans, stone tools, hunter-gatherer lifestyle | ~3.3 million BCE – 10,000 BCE |
| Mesolithic (Middle Stone Age) | Transition period; improved tools and early settlements | ~10,000 BCE – 8000 BCE |
| Neolithic (New Stone Age) | Agriculture, domestication of animals, permanent villages | ~8000 BCE – 3300 BCE |
| Copper Age (Chalcolithic) | First use of copper alongside stone tools | ~5000 BCE – 3300 BCE |
| Bronze Age | Bronze tools and weapons; rise of cities, writing, trade | ~3300 BCE – 1200 BCE |
| Iron Age | Iron tools and weapons become dominant | ~1200 BCE – 500 CE |
| Classical Age | Development of major civilizations, philosophy, science | ~500 BCE – 500 CE |
| Middle Ages | Medieval kingdoms, feudal systems, strong religious influence | ~500 – 1500 |
| Renaissance | Revival of art, science, and learning in Europe | ~1300 – 1700 |
| Industrial Age | Mechanization, factories, steam power | ~1760 – 1900 |
| Modern / Atomic Age | Rapid technology, electricity, nuclear energy | ~1900 – 1990 |
| Digital / Information Age | Computers, internet, data-driven society | ~1990 – present |
