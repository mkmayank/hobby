* Kyrgyz
    * turkic word => we are forty
    * believed to refer to forty clans of `Manas`
        * a legendary hero who united 40 regional clans

## **Geography**
* farthest from the sea than any other individual country
* all rivers flow into closed drainage systems which do not reach the sea
* mountain ranges of `Tian Shan` covers over 80% of the country
* `Issyk-Kul Lake` largest lake in the country
    * second largest mountain lake in the world
* `Jengish Chokusu` 7,439 m is the highest point
* less than 8% of the land is cultivated
* principal river is `Kara Darya`
    * across border in Uzbekistan it meets another major Kyrgyz river, the `Naryn`
