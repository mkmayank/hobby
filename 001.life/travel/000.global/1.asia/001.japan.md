# Japan

----
* population -> `12.3 Cr`
* Capital -> `Tokyo`
* area -> `377,976 sq km` 1.55x of UP area
* west -> `sea of japan`
* north -> `sea of Okhotsk`
* south -> `east china sea`
* east -> `pacific ocean`
* Japanese archipelago
    * 4 major islands +
    * 14121 smaller islands
    * ~ 260 are inhabited
* 3rd largest island country in the world ( after Indonesia and madagascar)



---

## Tourism
1. `Shinkansen` (bullet train) from Tokyo to Osaka
2. stay in `Ryokans` (traditional Japanese inns)
3. try Japanese `onsen` (hot-spring)
