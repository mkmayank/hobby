### **Chenab (chandra bhaga)**
* formed by union of two headwaters at **`Tandi`** , 8km of **`Keylong`** in **`Lahaul and Spiti`** district of Himachal Pradesh
    1. **`Chandra`**
        * originates from glaciers east
            * **`Bara-lacha la pass`**
            * **`Chandra taal`**
    2. **`Bhaga`**
        * originates from **`Surya Taal lake`**
            * situated at few km west of **`Bara-lacha la pass`**
* called as `Asikni` in the `Rigveda` and to ancient greek
    * meant that it was seen to have dark-coloured waters
* `mahabharata` name was `chandrabhaga`
* flows from himachal to jammu to pakistan emptying into Sutlej forming `Panjnad river`
    * formed by successive confluence or merger of
        1. Jhelum
        2. Chenab
        3. Rabi
        4. Beas
        5. Sutlej
---

### **Ravi**
* known as `Iravati` or `Purushni` in Vedas
* known as `Hydraotes` to ancient greek
* part of Battle of ten kings fought on this river
* rises in `Bara Bhangal` , `Kangra`
* drains in arabian sea through Indus river in Pakistan

---

### **Beas**
* rises in `Rohtang pass` in `kullu`
* to sutlej in punjab
* marks the easternmost border of `Alexander the Great` conquests in 326BC
    * river created problem in Alexander's invasion in India
    * his troops mutinied here, refusing to go any further
    * had been away from home for eight years
    * Alexander shut himself in his tent for 3 days
        * but his men did not change their desires, he gave in
            * raising twelve colossal altars to mark the limit and glory of his expedition
* mahabharata -> `Veda Vyasa` - eponym of river Beas
    * said to have created from its source lake `beas kund`
