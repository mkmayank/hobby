## History
* Lepchas - earliest inhabitants of Sikkim
* Limbus and Magars also lived in the inaccessible parts of West and South
    * as early as the Lepchas lived in the East and North districts
* Buddhist saint Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche)
    * said to have passed throuh the land in 8th century
    * introduced Buddhism
    * foretold the era of monarchy that would arrive centuries later
* native sikkimese consists of Bhutias (who migrated from Kham district of Tibet in 14th century )

## political
* kingdom of sikkim - Dremoshong
    * Absolute monarchy until 1975
    * Chogyal was the absolute monarch of Sikkim from 1642 to 1973
        * Bhutanese domination (1680/1700–1792)
        * Nepalese domination (1776–1792)
        * Nepalese-Bhutanese presence (1792–1816)
        * British presence (1816–1890)
        * Protectorate of the British Empire (1861–1947)
        * Protectorate of India (1950–1975)
* 1975 Sikkimese monarchy referendum
    * make the Sikkim the 22nd state of India
    * approved by 97.55% (~59K people)

#### Chogyal Monarchy
* founded by Phuntsog Namgyal
    * fifth generation descendant of Guru Tashi
        * a prince of Minyak House who came to Sikkim from the Kham province of Tibet

## Geology
* hills consists of gneiss and schist
* soil is coarse, with large concentrations of iron oxide
    * neutral to acidic
    * lacks organic and mineral nutrients
    * this type of soil tends to support evergreen and deciduous forests.
* landslides are frequent

---

## Climate
* five seasons
    1. winter
    2. summer
    3. spring
    4. autumn
    5. monsoon
* ranges from sub-tropical in south to tundra in north
* temperature seldom exceeds 28 °C
* avg annual temperature is around 18 °C
* receives regular snowfall
* snowline ranges from 6,100m in south to 4,900m in north
* temp in mountains can drop to as low as −40 °C in winter
* tundra-type region in north is snowbound for 4months every year
    * tmp drops below 0°C every night
* record of longest continuous rain is 11 days
