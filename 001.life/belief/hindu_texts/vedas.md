* श्रमण a person who
    * labours, toils (कड़ा परिश्रम) or
    * exerts themselves for some higher or religious purpose
    * or seeker who performs austerity , ascetic
    * includes primarily Jainism , Buddhism

> संन्‍यासी -> ascetic - धार्मिक कारणों से भौतिक सुखों से विमुख

> संयम, कठोरता, आडंबरहीनता -> austerity

---

* आश्रम is a system of stages of life discussed in Hindu texts of the ancient and medieval eras
* The four asramas are
    1. ब्रह्मचर्य (student),
    2. गृहस्थ (householder),
    3. वानप्रस्थ (forest walker/forest dweller),
    4. संन्यास (renunciate)

---

* Vedas are श्रुति ( what is heard ) vs other religious texts which are स्मृति ( what is remembered )
