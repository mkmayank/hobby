## Hinduism

* umbrella term for a range of Indian regligious and spritual traditions (`सम्प्रदाय`)
    * unified by adherence (निष्‍ठा) to concept of `धर्म`
        * maintaining an order `ऋत` : (meaning order, rhythm , truth (from `ऋग्वेद`)) through rituals and righteous living
            * which regulates and cooridnates the operation of the universe and everything within it

---

* Hindu is exonym
* `सनातन धर्म` is endonym and `Vedic dharma` too

---

* hinduism entails diverse system of thoughts
    * marked by range of shared concepts that discuss theology, mythology

> theology : study of divine, discipline of belief

> mythology : stories, doesn't demand faith

major denominations
1. `शैवसंप्रदायः`
    * shiva
    * second largest hindu sect . 2020 - 385 million
2. `शाक्तसम्प्रदायः`
    * deity or metaphysical reality is considered metaphorically to be a woman
3. `वैष्णवसम्प्रदायः`
    * vishnu
    * largest hindu sect. 2020 - 399 million
4. `स्मार्त`
    * rejects theistic sectarianism or
        * says no to picking one god over others
    * is notable for the domestic worship of five shrines with five deities
    * all treated as equal
        1. Ganesha
        2. Shiva
        3. Shakti
        4. Vishnu
        5. Surya
    * regards Adi Shankara (8th c. CE) as founder or reformer

> theistic : relating to or characterized by belief in the existence of a god or gods

six `आस्तिक` schools (system of ideas) of hindu philosophy that recognise the authority of the Vedas
1. `सांख्य`
    * sees the universe as a duality
        * `पुरुष` (consciousness) and `प्रकृति` (emotions) (prakriti)
        * it’s about realizing that true self (the spirit) is separate from the material world
2. `योग`
    * built on `सांख्य` ideas, but more practical
    * it’s about disciplining mind and body through practices like meditation and physical exercises
3. `न्याय`
    * school of logic that uses debate and evidence to understand reality and achieve liberation through clear knowledge
    * epistemology accepts four out of six `प्रमाण` as reliable means of gaining knowledge
        1. `प्रत्यक्ष` (perception)
        2. `अनुमान` (inference)
        3. `उपमान` (comparison and analogy)
        4. `शब्द` (testimony of past or present reliable experts)
    * rejects
        1. `अर्थापत्ति` (postulation)
        2. `अनुपलब्धि` (non-perception, negative/cognitive proof)
4. `वैशेषिक`
    * views all physical objects as made of atoms `परमाणु` with experiences arising from their qualities, arrangements, and cosmic forces
    * aiming for liberation through understanding reality, and complements `न्याय`
5. `मीमांसा`
    * means "reflection" or "critical investigation"
    * advocates `धर्म` meant rituals and social duties - not Devas, or Gods, because Gods existed only in name
    * also held that Vedas are "eternal, author-less, infallible"
    * vedic rituals are of primary importance and merit
6. `वेदान्त`
    * means conclusion of the Vedas
    * dives into the deeper meaning of the `उपनिषद`
