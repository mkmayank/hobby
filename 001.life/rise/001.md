## Food chain looks cruel
One organism survives by consuming another. But nature also contains enormous cooperation:

* cells cooperate to form your body
* humans cooperate to form societies
* bees cooperate in colonies
* plants and fungi cooperate
* parents sacrifice for offspring

evolution does not select only **exploitation**.

It selects whatever **survives and reproduces successfully**.

Sometimes that is competition. Sometimes cooperation.

> Capitalism : **market rewards what people buy, not necessarily what benefits humanity.**
